How the prompter reads your script
  • # Heading (or Word Heading 1/2/3) starts a new section.
  • PDFs: bigger text becomes a section heading. Check the result before saving — PDFs don’t store headings, so the app works them out from text size.
  • No headings? Short lines (8 words or fewer, no ending punctuation) become sections.
  • [Walk to the left] — anything in square brackets is a stage cue.
  • [Slide 5 · Matthew 25:14] — shows a gold “▶ Slide 5” chip.
  • > Scripture — lines starting with > are shown as scripture.
  • Several scripts in one file? Section breaks in Word or Google Docs (Insert → Break → Section break) split it into separate scripts. When pasting, put a line === SECTION BREAK === between them.
